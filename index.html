The Project Story

From fragmented notices to a Smart Knowledge Graph.

⚠️ The Problem

Information Black Holes: Students miss critical deadlines because notices are trapped in scanned PDFs and image files.

💡 The Vision

Digital Archaeology: A system that "excavates" this buried data, treating every notice as a historical artifact to be indexed.

🛠️ The Tech

We combine OCR to read images, LLMs to understand context, and Vector Search for instant answers.

🚀 The Model

A fully functional engine proving that university data can be as accessible as a Google search. Live now.
